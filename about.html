ℹ️ About DigiLibrary

Learn more about our digital library system

🎯 Our Mission

DigiLibrary is a simple web-based digital library management system designed to help students and individuals organize and manage their book collections. It provides an easy-to-use interface for adding, searching, and browsing books.


📋 Features

  • 📖 Add Books — Add new books with title, author, category, year, and ISBN
  • 🔍 Search & Filter — Find books quickly by title, author, or category
  • 📊 Library Stats — View total books, available count, and categories
  • 💾 Local Storage — All data is saved in your browser automatically
  • 🗑️ Delete Books — Remove books you no longer need
  • 📱 Responsive — Works on desktop and mobile devices
